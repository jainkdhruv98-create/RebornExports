Pharmaceutical Exports

The Product Range We Cater

Our portfolio comprises 54 formulations spanning single-entity therapy, fixed-dose combinations, pediatric formulations and broader antivirals — covering first-line through advanced HIV/AIDS treatment regimens, as well as influenza and Hepatitis C support. Manufactured by Mcneil & Argus Pharmaceuticals Limited, a WHO-GMP & ISO 9001:2015 certified facility.

Single Entity ART

17 FORMULATIONS

Our single-entity range covers every major antiretroviral class — Raltegravir, Darunavir, Dolutegravir, Rilpivirine, Tenofovir (TAF and TDF), Ritonavir, Abacavir, Atazanavir, Efavirenz and Nevirapine — giving procurement teams the flexibility to build custom regimens or supplement therapy already in place.

Combination ART (Fixed-Dose)

22 FORMULATIONS

Fixed-dose combinations are the core of our portfolio. These 2- and 3-drug combinations, including WHO-preferred single-tablet regimens such as TDF/3TC/DTG, are designed to reduce pill burden and improve adherence — a priority for national treatment programs operating at scale.

Pediatric Formulations & Oral Solutions

10 FORMULATIONS

Ten formulations dedicated to pediatric care: weight-banded tablets and oral liquids covering Zidovudine, Lamivudine, Abacavir, Nevirapine, Lopinavir/Ritonavir and Efavirenz, alongside Oseltamivir suspensions. Several support standard PMTCT protocols in maternal and neonatal care settings.

Antivirals (Non-HIV)

5 FORMULATIONS

Beyond HIV, we supply Favipiravir, Ribavirin and three strengths of Oseltamivir — supporting seasonal and pandemic influenza response, outbreak stockpiling, and combination therapy for chronic Hepatitis C.
